\section{RQ2: Anchoring to clean-code behaviour}
\label{sec:loss}

The anchored objective (Section~\ref{sec:setup}, Eq.~\ref{eq:paired_consistency}) supervises the
student on obfuscated input against a frozen clean-code-tuned teacher reading the unobfuscated
parent, on exactly the rows breadth trains on.

\textbf{The ablation is the contribution.} Replacing the clean-code-tuned teacher with an untuned
base model makes the arm \textbf{collapse} ($-8.98$ [$-11.44$, $-6.43$] against breadth on the unseen
family); replacing it with the breadth model makes the arm \textbf{inherit breadth's tax} ($-4.20$
[$-6.27$, $-2.14$] against the clean-code teacher) while matching on seen conditions. Across all
three teachers the student lands at roughly the teacher's unseen-family level plus one point. The
seen-condition gain comes from the supervised term and is teacher-independent; the unseen-family
behaviour is \emph{distilled from the teacher}. The method is ``distil from a clean-code-\emph{tuned}
model on the clean parent'', and we name it as such.

\textbf{What it repairs, and on which models.} Table~\ref{tab:rq3_panel} reads all eight models from
one evaluation phase; every previously published contrast recomputed through it falls inside the new
interval (largest disagreement 0.85 points), so a departure is a property of the model. Breadth's
unseen-family tax has the same sign on every model, six significantly, across four lineages and
7B--34B. The repair holds on six of eight models and \textbf{reverses on Granite-3.1-8B}, where the
anchored arm is worse than breadth on the unseen family. ``Anchoring pays no clean-code cost'' holds
on five of eight. Every failure falls outside the lineage the findings were discovered on, where all
three are four-of-four; with $n = 8$ and vendor confounded with lineage (Fisher's exact $p = 0.0714$
one-sided) that is a warning that single-lineage evaluation would have hidden all of it, not a claim
about lineages.

\begin{takeaway}{Takeaway: RQ2}
Distilling from a clean-code-\emph{tuned} teacher on the unobfuscated parent, on exactly the rows
breadth trains on, removes breadth's unseen-family tax on six of eight models and reverses it on one
(Granite-3.1-8B), at no clean-code cost on five of eight. The teacher ablation shows where the effect
lives: the seen-condition gain is teacher-independent, the unseen-family behaviour is the teacher's.
\end{takeaway}
