\section{Every method against every obfuscation}
\label{sec:master}

Table~\ref{tab:master_abs_codellama7b} is the reference the research questions draw on: the raw
accuracy of every method on every condition and stack for CodeLlama-7B, the one model on which every
arm exists, forward and, for the ladder, backward. Every number is a strict exact-match accuracy on the
held-out programs of that condition; nothing is normalised. The untuned model scores 0.257 on clean code,
0.168--0.207 on the single seen transforms, 0.119 on the unseen family and 0.089--0.186 on the stacks.
Every tuned arm lands between 0.35 and 0.43 on the single seen transforms and their stacks; the
columns separate on the unseen family (0.231 breadth, 0.269 clean LoRA, 0.286 anchored, 0.316 family)
and on the stacks that contain it. The backward block is read on this model only, because the one-shot
inverse template collapses the format on most other panel models; the per-model grids for the whole
panel, and the routing and merging arms on every model that has them, are in
Appendices~\ref{app:master} and \ref{app:routing}.
