\section{Every intervention against every obfuscation}
\label{sec:master}

Table~\ref{tab:master_summary} puts every intervention against every kind of obfuscation across the
panel; Appendix~\ref{app:master} gives the per-model grids. Each cell carries the raw accuracy, the
change against the untuned model, and that accuracy as a percentage of the untuned model's clean-code
accuracy --- how much of the original the intervention brings back. The untuned model keeps 66--81\%
of its clean accuracy on single seen transforms, 46\% on the unseen family and 35--58\% on depth-3/4
stacks (CodeLlama-7B). Every intervention more than restores clean accuracy on seen transforms and
seen stacks, so those columns do not separate them; the unseen-family columns do: breadth 91\%,
clean-code control 102\%, anchored 99\%, family 128\% of clean accuracy (panel mean), in the same
order on the stacks containing the family. Backward cells survive the format gate almost only on
CodeLlama-7B, where the family adapter never loses backward accuracy, anchoring stays within 2.3
points of the untuned model and breadth loses up to 3.9.
