Table~\ref{tab:main} reports each adaptation as a mean over a block of conditions. This appendix gives
the cells those means are taken over: every method against every obfuscation, one table per model, for
the eight panel models. Each cell reads
\emph{output prediction}\,/\,\emph{input prediction}. Bold rows are the block means, and an accuracy is
coloured where it sits above or below the untuned model in the same direction; a red number carrying a
dagger is red for the other reason, its format-failure rate is above 0.25 and the accuracy beneath it
is not a fair reading of the arm. CodeLlama-7B (Table~\ref{tab:master_abs_codellama7b}) is the one
model on which every arm exists.
