\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\textbf{The held-out family is a proxy.} Every unseen-family number is measured on X1, a trainable
sibling of the quarantined family H1, whose budget was spent under a pre-registered two-pass
discipline; X1 reproduces H1's difficulty at $r = 0.9992$ and transfer between them was lossless
(0.08 points) for arms trained on the sibling. It is a single family pair; a second hard family with
a different surface would settle whether ``family'' means ``surface family''. \textbf{Coverage is not
uniform.} Conditions cover different program sets; every contrast runs on a subset recorded before
evaluation, and where the intersection moves an estimate we report both. \textbf{Eight models is a
small sample for a claim about models}; the panel's strongest non-lineage model is also the one an
untuned capability screen would have discarded. \textbf{The backward grade is permissive.} Inversion
is many-to-one, so a produced call is correct if it returns the gold value; 15\,\% of items have a
trivial gold that a well-formed guess can hit, and on one model (Llama-3.1-8B) that accounts for
almost the whole of an apparently strong untuned backward score. Appendix~\ref{app:absmaster} reports
exact-argument recovery alongside execution grading, and the merge's advantage survives the stricter
grade where we can measure it. \textbf{Format gating removes cells, not at random.} A cell whose
format-failure rate exceeds 0.25 is excluded from every mean; those cells concentrate in the backward
direction and on the weaker models, so the backward block is read on fewer cells than the forward one
and its means are correspondingly less stable. Gated cells are marked rather than hidden.
\textbf{Merge density was tuned off-panel.} The DARE-TIES density of 0.5 was selected by a sweep on
the 1.5\,B pilot model and applied unchanged to all eight panel models; it is therefore not fitted to
the panel, but neither is it tuned for it. \textbf{Instrument failures are reported, not omitted.}
The causal attention instrument is inert (an identifier knockout moves the gold log-probability by
under 0.5\,\% everywhere), so any attention account would be correlational; we do not make one.
\textbf{Single language.} Every headline is Python; the JavaScript corpus exists but cannot be
regenerated on our cluster.
