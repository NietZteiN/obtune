\section{Threats to Validity}

\textbf{The held-out family is a proxy.} Every unseen-family number is measured on X1, a trainable
sibling of the quarantined family H1, whose budget was spent under a pre-registered two-pass
discipline; X1 reproduces H1's difficulty at $r = 0.9992$ and transfer between them was lossless
(0.08 points) for arms trained on the sibling. It is a single family pair; a second hard family with
a different surface would settle whether ``family'' means ``surface family''. \textbf{Coverage is not
uniform.} Conditions cover different program sets; every contrast runs on a subset recorded before
evaluation, and where the intersection moves an estimate we report both. \textbf{Eight models is a
small sample for a claim about models}, and Section~\ref{sec:rq3} declines to make one; the panel's
strongest non-lineage model is also the one an untuned capability screen would have discarded.
\textbf{Coverage of the baselines.} Merging is measured forward only, on four models
(Table~\ref{tab:rq1_merge_panel}); routing is measured on seen stacks on one model, so its verdict
rests on matching breadth where the two are measured together; the direction ratio is one model deep,
because the one-shot inverse template collapses the format on most other panel models.
\textbf{Instrument failures are reported, not omitted.} The causal attention instrument is inert (an
identifier knockout moves the gold log-probability by under 0.5\,\% everywhere), so the attention
account is correlational; the weakest-link test in Section~\ref{sec:rq2} returned undecided.
\textbf{Single language.} Every headline is Python; the JavaScript corpus exists but cannot be
regenerated on our cluster.
