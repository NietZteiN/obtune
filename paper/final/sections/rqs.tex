\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:rqs}

We evaluate three research questions:

\begin{itemize}

    \item \textbf{RQ1: Does specialist merging improve reasoning across clean
    and obfuscated code?}
    We evaluate clean code and individual obfuscations to determine whether
    combining specialists improves performance over the untuned model across
    both settings.

    \item \textbf{RQ2: Do adaptations learned from individual obfuscations
    transfer to stacked obfuscation?}
    We evaluate unseen stacks of increasing depth to determine whether
    separately learned adaptations remain effective as multiple obfuscations
    are combined.

    \item \textbf{RQ3: How well do adapted models generalize beyond the
    training setting?}
    We evaluate a held-out obfuscation family and reverse reasoning, neither of
    which is used during adaptation.

\end{itemize}
