# 2026-09-13 — The a30 partition holds 7–8B models only

**Thread:** setup · **Status:** fixed in the drain script; two jobs requeued · **Related:** [`2026-08-28_juno-migration.md`](2026-08-28_juno-migration.md)

`tr_L1r_codellama-13b` (393483) died two minutes into training on `g-02-01`:

```
torch.OutOfMemoryError: CUDA out of memory. Tried to allocate 136.00 MiB.
GPU 0 has a total capacity of 23.49 GiB of which 36.25 MiB is free.
```

CLAUDE.md §1 calls `a30` "the closest analogue to the old A6000s; cheap smoke tests" and warns only
that the **MIG slices** (`a30-2.12gb`, `a30-4.6gb`) are too small for 1.5B training. The full cards
are 23.5 GB, which holds a 7–8B model in bf16 with LoRA and does not hold 13B (~26 GB of weights
before activations), 12B, 15B or 34B. Nothing said so, and the queue drain was handing a30 whatever
sat at the head of the priority order.

**Fix.** `runs/probe/drain_queue.sh` no longer drains a30 by priority. It submits named manifests
whose model is one of `codellama-7b`, `llama31-8b`, `codegemma-7b`, `granite31-8b`; h100 and h200
take anything. Requeued: `tr_L1r_codellama-13b` (this OOM) and `ev_depth_gemma3-12b` (12.2B, cancelled
off a30 before it started and before it could waste the slot).

**Sizes, for the next person.** Full a30 23.5 GB: 7–8B yes, 12B+ no. h100 `g-04-02` 4×80 GB and
`g-05-01` NVL 94 GB: everything including 34B. `g-06-01` is MIG slices (47 GB) — trains, but does not
serve vLLM, and is auto-excluded for `obtune.eval_vllm`. h200 141 GB: everything, capped at obtune's
share of 2 in the juno pool. 34B additionally needs `--mem 128G` on the host.
