# 2026-09-13 — CodeLlama-34B is h200-only, for serving as well as training

**Thread:** setup · **Status:** fixed; manifests parked and routed · **Related:** [`2026-09-13_codegemma-vocab-breaks-the-size-rule.md`](2026-09-13_codegemma-vocab-breaks-the-size-rule.md)

`ev_depth_codellama-34b` failed on `g-04-02` (4 × H100 80 GB, one card allocated) after three minutes:

```
ValueError: No available memory for the cache blocks.
Try increasing `gpu_memory_utilization` when initializing the engine.
```

34B in bf16 is ~68 GB of weights. At the project's `gpu_memory_utilization: 0.85` an 80 GB card offers
68 GB, so the weights consume the entire budget and nothing is left for a KV cache. Raising the
fraction does not fix it — the remaining 12 GB is the card's own overhead plus what the engine needs
for activations, and the published 34B evaluations all ran on **h200** (141 GB), where 0.85 leaves
roughly 52 GB of cache.

I had already pinned 34B **training** to h200 at 128 G earlier today after cancelling two jobs off a30
and h100. The same bound applies to **serving**, and I did not draw it then; the depth eval went to
h100 with the rest of the evaluation batch.

**Fix.** 34B manifests are parked in `runs/manifest/queued_34b/`, which the drain's `--queued` passes
cannot see (`QUEUED.glob("*.json")` is not recursive), and are submitted one at a time by name to h200
with `--mem 128G`. Seven manifests are parked: two evals and five specialist trainings.

**Per-partition placement, consolidated.**

| workload | a30 23.5 GB | h100 80 GB (`g-04-02`) / NVL 94 GB (`g-05-01`) | h200 141 GB |
|---|---|---|---|
| train 7B (32k vocab) | yes | yes | yes |
| train 8B (128k vocab) | yes | yes | yes |
| train 8.5B (256k vocab, CodeGemma) | **no** — fp32 logits | yes | yes |
| train 12–15B | **no** | yes | yes |
| train 34B | **no** | **no** (needs 128 G host too) | yes |
| serve ≤7B under vLLM | yes | yes | yes |
| serve 8.5B+ under vLLM | **no** — KV cache | yes | yes |
| serve 34B under vLLM | **no** | **no** — no room for KV | yes |

`g-06-01` is MIG slices: it trains, and it cannot serve vLLM at all (auto-excluded).
