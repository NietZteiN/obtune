- **Observations:**
  - X1 is the one lever that touches the held-out claim, so its discipline is written down before any
    number exists: X1 is reported in its own namespace, never pooled with the six-condition grid, and
    its H1 read is **one** `final_eval` spend at campaign end together with the winner (agreed with the
    user 2026-09-05). If `tuned_X1` lifts H1, the finding is "the family transfers, the surface does
    not need to match" — which is the semantic-invariance claim at family granularity. If it does not,
    the specialists' H1 band (0.252–0.283) was the ceiling for single-transform training and the
    memorisation verdict extends from transform to family.
  - The X1 site bar (3) is deliberately low: the corpus is short programs (median ~300 tokens) and most
    have ≤3 arithmetic sites. A bar of 5 would have gated far fewer than the 68 % the testset shows.
    The `int`-guard means every helper call also carries a runtime type test — a model that learned to
    "see through" `_ar_p` must also learn that it is the identity on non-ints; that is part of what
    makes X1 a family sibling rather than a syntax swap.
  - Build-log `SyntaxWarning: "is" with 'int' literal` lines come from parent programs that already
    compare with `is`; the gate compiles them in both the `<variant>` and `<x1>` namespaces, so each
    warns twice. Cosmetic.
  - The pytest job's tracebacks print `/data/jvl210002/my_downloads/obtune/...` — stale `__pycache__`
    `co_filename`s from the old host, not a path leak in the code.
